Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

CyberAlvi ("we", "us", "our") operates the Operixa Field mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our services:

Personal Information

  • Account data: name, email address, and role within your organization, provided during account creation.
  • Authentication data: login credentials used to secure your account.

Data Collected Through Device Permissions

Permission Purpose
Location (precise & approximate) Geotagging field data collection entries and site visits for monitoring purposes.
Camera Capturing photos as evidence during field visits and surveys.
Microphone Recording audio notes during field data collection.
Notifications Sending alerts about synchronization status and assigned tasks.
Internet & Network Synchronizing collected data with the server and checking connectivity status.
Storage (Android 9 and below) Saving data locally when the device is offline for later synchronization.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not access or collect SMS messages, call logs, contacts, or any telephony data. Permissions for these are explicitly removed from our application.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide field data collection, monitoring, and evaluation services.
  • To synchronize data between your device and your organization's server.
  • To verify the location and authenticity of field visits.
  • To send notifications about tasks, data synchronization, and system updates.
  • To improve the functionality and user experience of the application.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data collected through Operixa Field is stored on servers managed by your organization or on our secure infrastructure. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Secure authentication mechanisms.
  • Local data encryption on the device.
  • Role-based access controls.

Data collected offline is stored securely on your device and transmitted to the server when connectivity is restored.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Within your organization: Data is accessible to authorized members of your organization according to their assigned roles.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Service providers: With trusted service providers who assist in operating our infrastructure, subject to confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services to your organization. When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable regulations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Operixa Field is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
